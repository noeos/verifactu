| Daniel David | `@ddavid07` | product, regulation, architecture, security, releases and documentation |

Privileges follow least privilege and require 2FA. A future maintainer must have documented responsibility, reviewed signing identity and enough recovery knowledge to operate the repository without sharing personal credentials.

Changes to maintainers, allowed signers or release reviewers are security-sensitive decisions and require signed history and updated GitHub audit evidence.
