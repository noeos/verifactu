# Assurance model

Claims identify subject/scope/edition/environment/time and required positive,
boundary and falsifying evidence. Levels are owner self-assessment, automated
tool, independent implementation, external professional and authority observation;
none inherits another's scope.

Confidence weighs authority, independence, completeness, freshness and adversarial
power while preserving uncertainty. Critical claims require independent challenge
and full populations. Missing/contradictory evidence blocks; aggregate scores and
badges cannot compensate. Conclusions and residual limitations are reproducible.
