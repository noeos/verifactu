# Security and privacy audit evidence

Evidence closes asset/flow/threat/control/abuse/privacy mappings, behavioral
negatives, XML/PKI/network/secrets/tenant/resource controls, SAST/CodeQL/secrets/
OSV/npm/Scorecard and vulnerability/incident status.

Reports bind tool/database/config/SHA and distinguish zero findings from failure.
Data minimization, lawful purpose/roles/retention/breach decision and restricted
evidence access are inspected. Applicable critical/high or unknown critical
exposure blocks release.
