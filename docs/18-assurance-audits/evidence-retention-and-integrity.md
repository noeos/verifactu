# Evidence retention and integrity

Retention is class/subject/purpose/legal/support based: regulatory records,
release/source/declaration, security embargo, performance raw, CI diagnostic and
personal support data differ. No global duration or indefinite convenience copy.
Legal hold and tax evidence override ordinary deletion; privacy restriction/
minimization remains enforced.

Digests, signatures/attestations, immutable storage, access logs, redundant
encrypted copies and periodic restore/readability verify integrity. Historic
schema/tool/trust roots migrate by linked derivative without altering originals.
Authorized deletion records basis/scope and proves no active higher obligation.
