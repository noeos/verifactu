# Internal audit plan

Audits run per material PR, candidate, stable/post-publication, quarterly controls
and incident/change. Scope states criteria, population, procedure/tool, competence,
self-assessment limitation, evidence, findings and follow-up.

Required populations—sources, critical controls, packages, checks and 84 findings—
are complete, not sampled. Lower-risk sampling records population/method/seed/error.
Auditor cannot rewrite raw evidence or waive law; unresolved critical issue blocks.
