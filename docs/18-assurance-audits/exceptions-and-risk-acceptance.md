# Exceptions and risk acceptance

`EXC-*` names exact rule/subject/reason, authority, inherent/residual risk,
compensating control/evidence, start/expiry, release applicability and removal.
It cannot waive law, signatures/DCO, main/tag immutability, secret protection,
critical integrity or truthful claims.

Expiry/changed premise automatically blocks. Stable `1.0.0` has zero temporary
shortcut exceptions; any accepted residual risk is explicit in dossier/public
limitations where relevant. Closure proves control restored, not record deletion.
