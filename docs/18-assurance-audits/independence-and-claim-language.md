# Independence and claim language

Evidence declares owner/tool/independent/professional/authority class, competence,
conflict, subject/scope/date and limitations. “Certified”, “AEAT-approved”,
“legally compliant”, “secure” and “complete” require their exact authorized basis;
test portal response, badge or internal audit cannot supply it.

Public claim registry generates README/release/dossier phrases and rejects
unscoped superlatives, stale versions and omitted limitations. Negative examples
prove claim lint detects scope inflation.
