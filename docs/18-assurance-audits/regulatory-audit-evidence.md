# Regulatory audit evidence

Audit current legal/applicability/CRA review, complete source byte/dependency
manifest, edition generation, atomic requirement/interpretation trace, official/
independent vectors, external AEAT observations and declaration handoff.

Every legal/public claim cites exact authority/effective date and technical proof.
Ambiguity, unavailable source/portal or professional-review limitation is retained.
No internal audit certifies the integrated SIF or AEAT approval.
