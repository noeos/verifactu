# Risk assessment method

Record asset/objective, cause/event/consequence, inherent likelihood/impact,
velocity, detectability, uncertainty, affected scope, owner, controls, residual
risk, trigger/indicator, contingency, evidence and review date. Impact separates
legal, fiscal integrity, privacy/security, availability, compatibility and trust.

Use ordinal criteria with definitions, not arithmetic precision. Unknown is
conservative. Critical legal/integrity/secret risk cannot be accepted for schedule;
aggregation preserves correlated/common-cause risks. Review monthly, at source/
architecture/dependency/incident change and every release.
