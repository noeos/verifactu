# Complete-product delivery principles

Delivery is dependency-driven, evidence-first and releasable only as the complete
product. Small reversible increments keep protected main correct but are not MVP,
beta scope or permission to defer a committed capability. Regulatory/security/
privacy/integrity gates dominate schedule.

Every change has owner, authority, contract, tests, risks and rollback. Dates are
forecasts, never exit evidence. Unknowns fail conservatively; automation cannot
interpret law or self-approve. Parallelism requires stable boundaries and no
shared unowned output.
