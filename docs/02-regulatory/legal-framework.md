## Core regulatory duties translated for planning

- Generate an alta automatically simultaneously with or immediately before
  issuing each applicable invoice.
- Generate later records for correction/annulment while preserving the original.
- Guarantee integrity, conservation, accessibility, legibility, traceability and
  inalterability, including complete dated chains and detection of alteration.
- Maintain continuous, secure, correct, integral, automatic, consecutive,
  instantaneous and reliable electronic-remission capability.
- Separate every taxpayer's records, sequences, mode and operation.
- Provide authorized consultation, secure export and inspection without exposing
  unrelated confidential non-patrimonial information.
- Add official hash to applicable alta/anulación records; apply signature/event
  obligations according to effective mode.
- Include applicable QR/URL and VERI*FACTU wording in invoice representation.
- Issue and preserve a responsible declaration for each produced/commercialized
  product version with the ordered official content.

These statements are planning summaries. Atomic requirements cite the exact
article/paragraph and technical edition; summaries never authorize omission.

## Producer versus user dates

Taxpayer-use deadlines do not erase the separate producer/commercializer duty to
offer adapted products following the order's entry into force, including
maintained multi-year contracts. Release readiness follows the stricter
applicable producer obligation, not merely the last taxpayer deadline.

## Privacy and confidentiality

RRSIF itself requires GDPR/LOPDGDD compliance when personal data is processed.
The product must enable data protection by design/default, purpose limitation,
minimization, integrity/confidentiality and accountability while also preserving
legally required fiscal records. The deploying controller determines lawful
basis and rights handling; the component enforces technical capability and safe
defaults.

## Legal validation limit

Final release requires a dated legal/applicability review of the source matrix
and declaration. That review does not replace technical tests, and technical
success does not constitute legal advice or AEAT homologation.
