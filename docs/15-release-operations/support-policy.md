# Support policy

Before GA publish every major's start/end, package/edition/profile/Engine/runtime/
OS/provider scope and functional/regulatory/security/historical-verification
level. Proposed minimum security support is five years, extended by applicable
CRA expected use, legal/contract or retained evidence duty after capacity review.

Security private channel, public synthetic bug channel, integration support and
legal/privacy contacts are distinct. Published acknowledgement/triage/mitigation
objectives are measured; legal deadlines prevail. Support never requests real
fiscal records/keys in public issues.

EOL notice includes reason, dates, migration, last safe version and archival
verification. Unsafe algorithm/edition/runtime may shorten creation support but
not erase history. Unsupported means no guarantee, not automatic data deletion.
