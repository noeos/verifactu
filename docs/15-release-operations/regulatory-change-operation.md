# Regulatory change operation

Daily bounded monitoring captures BOE/ELI, AEAT portal/FAQ/technical dependency
graph, EUR-Lex CRA/privacy and related adopted sources. Human review precedes
adoption. Byte and semantic diff maps affected requirements/editions/code/tests/
declarations/releases/support and effective deadline.

Critical obligation/hash/signature/endpoint/applicability change freezes affected
release/creation immediately; high schema/catalog/protocol change opens a new
edition and blocks promotion. Network/HTML/parse/permission failure is unknown.

Emergency work may shorten communication/cooling but not authority, independent
oracle, critical regression, signed PR/release or history. Consumers receive
scope/deadline/migration; old edition bytes/evidence remain verifiable.
