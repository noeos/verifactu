# Responsible declaration handoff

Generate official-order descriptors and values for component name/code/version,
hardware/software/functions, modality/multi-taxpayer/signature capabilities,
producer identity/address, applicable compliance statement, date/place and
authorized signature, all bound to edition/packages/dossier.

VeriFactu outputs component evidence, third-party declarations and a conspicuously
unsigned/unapproved draft for the integrated SIF producer. Facturacion's legally
responsible producer must complete deployment/components/identity, review current
law/evidence and authorize/sign/publish the final declaration.

Placeholders, stale source/review, unsupported exclusive VERI*FACTU claim,
missing component, version/digest mismatch or open contradiction fails generation.
Historical signed declarations remain directly accessible and unmodified.
