# Developer Certificate of Origin

This project uses the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/). Every commit must include a sign-off matching its author:

```text
Signed-off-by: Name <email@example.com>
```

The sign-off certifies that the contributor has the right to submit the contribution under the project license, has identified third-party material and has not knowingly included secrets, personal fiscal data or incompatible content.

It does not replace the cryptographic commit signature; both are required.
